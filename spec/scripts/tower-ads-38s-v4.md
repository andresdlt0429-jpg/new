Source: Google Drive, "Tower Ads One Pager — 38s Meta Ad (Activo Model V4)" (andy@dltmktg.com). Copied 2026-10-23; tables unchanged.

9:16 vertical · phone footage · outdoors, truck or house in frame · Target: exterior cleaning owners, $25–50K/mo

## THE SCRIPT

| TIME | LINE | ON SCREEN |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **0:00–0:03** HOOK | "If your an exterior cleaning doing 25 to 50 grand a month — listen up." | You, on camera, outdoors. Truck or a house behind you. Overlay: $25–50K/MO |
| **0:03–0:09** MONEY | "One month your booked the next your struggling to find work" | Cut to b-roll: a calendar with empty squares next to full ones. Overlay: $40K → $18K |
| **0:09–0:14** REFRAME | "It's not your work. It's that you're relying on word of " | Back to your face. Flat delivery. |
| **0:14–0:18** AUTHORITY | "I've knocked doors and cleaned roofs myself, so I know exactly what that swing feels like." | Real footage of you working a route, if you have it. |
| **0:18–0:25** OFFER | "Here's the deal — I run your ads, build your funnel, and follow up with every lead so nothing falls through." | Screen recordings: Ads Manager, a live ad, CRM firing a text. Fast cuts. |
| **0:25–0:31** KICKER | "And here's the kicker — if I don't book you the jobs, you don't pay me a dime." | Hard cut to your face. Slow down. Overlay: NO JOBS = NO PAY |
| **0:31–0:35** DIFFERENTIATION | "I'm not a marketer running ads for dentists and roofers. Pressure washing is all I do." | Tight on your face. Flat delivery, no smile. |
| **0:35–0:38** CTA | "Tap the link below — takes sixty seconds, and we'll see if you qualify. Five spots left this year." | Your face, then arrow to button. Overlay: 5 SPOTS LEFT |

## ⚠️ FLAG BEFORE YOU SHOOT

- The reframe and money beat now carry more of your VSL scripts' voice than Activo's. If you want it to feel more like Activo's original register, revert just those two beats.

## SHOOTING NOTES

- Hook and money beat need to land back to back without a breath.
- Reframe delivered flat, no smile.
- Kicker is the slowest moment in the ad. Hard cut, stop moving, deliver "you don't pay me a dime" with zero hedge in your voice.
- Real footage over stock wherever possible.
- Graphics fire three times: revenue overlay at 0:00, the $40K → $18K swing at 0:03, NO JOBS = NO PAY at 0:25, and 5 SPOTS LEFT at the CTA. Keep it to these — no extra kinetic text.
