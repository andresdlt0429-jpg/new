# Starting Out / Ready to Scale Ad: Single Ad Set CBO

Source: Google Drive, "Starting Out / Ready to Scale Ad: Single Ad Set CBO" (andy@dltmktg.com). Copied 2026-10-23; tables unchanged.

One 40-second ad for people just starting permanent lighting, run as a single ad set CBO with five hooks tested against one identical body.

## The hooks (one per ad)

Each hook flows straight into "Most people buy the lights…".

| # | Hook type (source) | Say | Show | Text |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **1** | Visual metaphor + prop shock | "This is most new permanent lighting businesses. Great lights. Nothing plugged in." | You holding a glowing strand, then you yank the plug and it goes dark | GREAT LIGHTS. NOTHING PLUGGED IN. |
| **2** | Silent killer | "The number one reason new lighting companies stall out after a few installs. And it's not the install." | A lit roofline at dusk that flickers, then cuts out | IT'S NOT THE INSTALL |
| **3** | Qualifier callout | "If you've done zero permanent lighting installs, or just a few, you need to hear this." | You walking toward camera, one lit house behind you, flat delivery | 0 TO 5 INSTALLS? LISTEN. |
| 4 | Reframe | "You think you need better installs. What you actually need is a phone that rings." | A strand in one hand, a silent phone in the other | BETTER INSTALLS DON'T RING YOUR PHONE |
| 5 | Comparison | "YouTube can teach you to install permanent lights in a weekend. It can't get you one customer." | An install tutorial on a phone, then a swipe to an empty call log | YOUTUBE TEACHES INSTALLS. NOT CUSTOMERS. |

## Confirm before filming

1. "4 new lighting companies a month" is your real limit.
2. Skyler is locked in for supplier pricing and install training.
3. No ladder scene with a buzzing phone (Ad Editor Brief rule).
