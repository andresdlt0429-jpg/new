## Meta policy scan

Checked against "Launchpad Ads: Policy-Safe One-Pagers" (Andy's Drive, 2026-10-16).

- MEDIUM: Hook "still got 20 grand a month" names the viewer's revenue. Meta's personal-attributes rule rejects ads that imply they know the viewer's financial situation. Safer: "Exterior cleaners who want more booked jobs."
- MEDIUM: "$20K A MONTH?" on screen is a money figure. Money on screen can read as an income claim.
- MEDIUM: "20 more jobs completely guaranteed ... every single month" is a guarantee. It is a jobs guarantee, not income, but the terms ("booked" means what, which conditions) must exist on the landing page.
