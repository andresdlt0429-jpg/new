# Ad Editor Brief: the structure every ad must follow

Source: Google Drive, "Ad Editor Brief — The Structure Every Ad Must Follow" (DLT Marketing production guide, andy@dltmktg.com). Copied 2026-10-24. Until `SOP-DLT-1-v2-video-standard.md` arrives, this is the engine's stand-in for SOP 2.2 (beat map) and SOP 4 (graphics). Where the SOP and this brief disagree, the SOP wins.

**Rule #1 · The script is law.** Do not rewrite lines, reorder beats, add claims, or add extra text. If a script sheet conflicts with this brief, the script sheet wins.

## Chapter 01 · The 9 beats every ad needs

| # | Beat | Its job | Typical time | On-screen text |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Hook + ICP callout | Name exactly who this is for. Trade plus a number. | 0:00–0:04 | Yes: the number |
| 2 | Problem | Name the pain in his words. Ego first, then the business. | 0:04–0:15 | Sometimes |
| 3 | Agitate / diagnosis | Explain WHY it keeps happening and what it costs. | 0:15–0:30 | Yes: short phrase |
| 4 | Solution + deliverables | What we built and what he gets. Fast list, pace speeds up. | 0:26–0:40 | Yes: rapid ticks |
| 5 | Future-pace | Show the after-state. The relief beat. | 0:36–0:46 | No |
| 6 | Risk reversal / guarantee | Take the risk off him. Slowest, heaviest moment. | 0:41–0:52 | Yes: oversized |
| 7 | Proof (when we have it) | A real result, screenshot or number. Never invent one. | Varies | Yes: real numbers only |
| 8 | CTA + qualifier | One clear action, with a reason to want it. | Last 8–12 s | Yes |
| 9 | Scarcity + logo | A real limit, then the logo card. Nothing after it. | Last 2–4 s | Yes + logo |

## Chapter 02 · What each beat must look like

| Beat | What the editor shows | Common mistakes |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 · Hook + ICP | Person on camera outdoors. Big text overlay of the revenue number or ICP label (like "$25K/MO"). Starts in the first frame. | Intro card, logo, "hey guys", music build-up, any delay. |
| 2 · Problem | B-roll that matches the words. Stay on the words. | Generic stock. Cutting away when the line is emotional. |
| 3 · Diagnosis | Simple graphic that shows the idea. Longest text hold in the ad besides the guarantee. | Fast cuts here. Quiet and cold. |
| 4 · Solution | Rapid ticks that match each word (OFFER / SCRIPTS / PAGE / ADS / CLOSER). About 1.5 s per cut. | Ticks that lag behind the words. Every cut lands on the word it shows. |
| 5 · Future-pace | Slow, calm b-roll. | Keeping the fast pace. This is where the ad exhales. |
| 6 · Guarantee | Hard cut to the speaker standing still. One oversized text line in one color, held a full beat. | Music swelling, animated text, or b-roll over it. Nothing should compete. |
| 7 · Proof | Only what the script provides. Readable for 2+ s. | Fake or stock results. |
| 8 · CTA | Speaker, form recording on top. Arrow to the button. Pulsing button text. | Multiple CTAs. |
| 9 · Scarcity + logo | Short text (like "5 SPOTS LEFT"), then the logo card in the last 2–3 s only. | Logo early, long outros. |

## Chapter 03 · Pacing and energy

| Beat | How it feels | Edit note |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Hook | Flat. Almost boring. | No added energy or effects. |
| Problem | Pointed, then frustrated | Slightly tighter cuts. |
| Diagnosis | Matter-of-fact, cold | Slow. Long holds. Quiet. |
| Solution / list | Fast, pace quickens | Shortest cuts in the ad. |
| Future-pace | Relief | Slow back down. |
| Guarantee | Hard, heavy | Stop everything. Hold. The slowest moment. |
| CTA | Warm, then commanding | Clear and simple. No new ideas. |

**The half-beat pause:** leave a half-beat of silence before the key lines the script marks ("this is for you", "all of it", "you don't pay a dime").

## Chapter 04 · Technical rules

- Format: 9:16; runtime per script sheet (usually 35–60 s); captions burned in, always on; clean audio first; music low or none under the speaker.
- Text on screen: only the text listed in the script sheet; big, one or two words, high contrast; numbers pop when spoken; no extra kinetic text or word-by-word effects; no emoji, stickers or memes.
- Footage: real over stock; outdoors, daytime; no offices, desks, cars or white walls; no intro card, no logo until the last seconds.
- Words: no profanity; no claims that aren't in the script; no invented numbers, results or testimonials; never a person on a ladder with a buzzing phone.

## Chapter 05 · Hook variants

Cut one version per hook using the same body; only the first 3–4 s change. Name files AdName_Hook1, AdName_Hook2.

## Chapter 06 · Before you send it back

- The first frame already has the speaker and the ICP text.
- All beats from the script sheet in order; nothing added or missing.
- Every on-screen number and phrase matches the script sheet exactly.
- The guarantee beat is the slowest moment, nothing competing.
- Solution ticks land on the words they show.
- Captions on, correct, readable on a phone.
- One CTA only. Logo only in the last seconds.
- Audio clean: no clipping, no music over the speaker.

## Chapter 07 · When the footage is bad

If the raw footage is missing something the script needs, tell us instead of covering it up. Use only what's in the folder.
